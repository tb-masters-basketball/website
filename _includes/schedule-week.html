{%- comment -%} One game day on the Schedule page: a sticky week heading, result cards for games already played, rows for the ones still to come or cancelled, and the bye. With highlight=true (the next game day) the upcoming games use the blue next-game panel. A game day where every game was cancelled is headed by its date and a "Cancelled" badge, with no week number. Usage: include schedule-week.html week=week stats=stats highlight=true {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign sw = include.week -%} {%- assign sw_stats = include.stats -%} {%- assign sw_played = 0 -%} {%- assign sw_open = 0 -%} {%- assign sw_cancelled = 0 -%} {%- for sw_g in sw.games %}{% if sw_g.played %}{% assign sw_played = sw_played | plus: 1 %}{% elsif sw_g.cancelled %}{% assign sw_cancelled = sw_cancelled | plus: 1 %}{% else %}{% assign sw_open = sw_open | plus: 1 %}{% endif %}{% endfor -%}

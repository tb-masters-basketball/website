{%- comment -%} How to add a team calendar, per device. Used inside calendar-links.html. {%- endcomment -%}
  • iPhone, iPad or Mac: tap Apple, then Subscribe. Changes show up within a few hours.
  • Outlook: the Apple link works in Outlook too: open it on a computer with Outlook and say yes to adding the calendar.
  • Google Calendar (Android phones too): tap Google, sign in if asked, then Add. It's easiest on a computer; the games then appear on your phone too. Google checks for changes on its own schedule, usually within a day.

How stats are counted

GP
Games played: the games where the player is on the score sheet.
PPG
Points per game: total points divided by games played, to one decimal.{% if ppg_min > 0 %} {{ ppg_rule }}{% endif %}
FT%
Free throws made divided by free throws attempted, to one decimal. A player needs at least {{ ft_min | default: 10 }} attempts to be ranked.
Subs
A sub's points count for them as a player and toward their team's score that day.
Techs
Technical fouls, from the T boxes on the score sheet. Unlike the other stats, they count for the whole season, playoffs included.
Flagrants
Flagrant fouls, from the Notes on the score sheet. Each one also counts as a personal foul. Like techs, they count for the whole season, playoffs included.
Playoffs
Kept separate. Playoff games never count in these stats (technical and flagrant fouls aside) or in the standings.
Source
The league's paper score sheets, typed into the site's game files. The numbers are recalculated every time the site is published.
